Domain Overview

Purpose

Banter Engine exists to keep football prediction leagues entertaining and engaging.

The system observes football matches, prediction results and league standings in order to detect moments that are worth highlighting, celebrating or mocking.

The objective is not to report football events.

The objective is to identify banter opportunities.

⸻

Core Concepts

Fact

A fact is an objective piece of information.

Examples:

* France defeated Spain 2-1
* Kevin gained 5 points
* Julien became first in the standings
* Pierre missed a prediction

Facts are neutral.

⸻

Banter Opportunity

A banter opportunity is a situation that deserves attention.

Examples:

* A leader is under pressure
* A player remains last for several rounds
* A prediction was saved by a late goal
* A perfect prediction was missed by a single goal

Banter opportunities are interpretations of facts.

⸻

Banter Message

A banter message is the final content produced by the system.

Examples:

* Humorous
* Dramatic
* Celebratory
* Provocative

The same opportunity may generate multiple messages.

⸻

Notification

A notification delivers a banter message to users.

Examples:

* Discord
* Ntfy
* Telegram

⸻

Domain Flow

Facts
→ Banter Opportunity Detection
→ Banter Message Generation
→ Notification Delivery

⸻

Non Goals

The project is not:

* A football score tracker
* A football statistics platform
* A generic notification system

The project exists to detect and amplify entertaining moments in prediction leagues.
