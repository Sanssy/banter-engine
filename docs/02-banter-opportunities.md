Banter Opportunities

This document describes the situations that Banter Engine considers worthy of generating notifications.

The purpose is not to report facts.

The purpose is to identify moments that are entertaining, dramatic, frustrating, surprising or worthy of celebration.

⸻

LeaderUnderPressure

Description

The current league leader is losing a significant part of their advantage.

Required Facts

* Current standings
* Previous standings

Detection Rule

The gap between first and second place decreases significantly.

Banter Angles

* Panic
* Pressure
* End of reign

Example

🚨 The gap is shrinking.

Kevin is no longer sleeping comfortably.
